    Sources of sound

    Pitch

    The large letter is the nearest note. Its colour shows how close you are, judged on your pitch averaged over about ½ s, so vibrato does not make it flicker.

    Green
    Within 10 cents of the note: in tune.
    Orange
    Within 30 cents: a little sharp or flat.
    Grey
    Between notes.

    A cent is 1/100 of a semitone.

    Vibrato

    Rate
    Pitch cycles per second (Hz). The shaded band, 5.5–6.7 Hz, is the range measured in ten celebrated opera singers (Prame 1994). The tick at 6 Hz is the rate listeners judged most appropriate (Anand et al. 2012).
    Extent
    How far the pitch swings on each side, in cents. Professional singers measured about ±34 to ±123 cents, averaging ±71 (Prame 1997). Very wide vibrato was judged less appropriate.

    Vibrato naturally speeds up near the end of a note. Very short notes are not long enough for a full cycle (about 0.17 s at 6 Hz).

    Formants

    A formant is a resonance of the vocal tract (the throat, mouth and lips). Opera singers can manipulate their vocal tract's resonance to change their sound.

    F0
    Not a formant: the fundamental frequency, the pitch you sing.
    F1
    Rises as the jaw opens: low for [i] and [u], high for [a].
    F2
    Rises as the tongue moves forward: high for [i], low for [u].
    F3
    Falls as the lips round.

    A formant is heard only where a harmonic of the voice (a whole-number multiple of F0) falls on it.

    F3 · ring

    Ring, also called the singer's formant or squillo (Italian for "ringing, clarion"), is the bright, carrying quality that lets a trained voice be heard over an orchestra. It forms when F3, F4 and F5 cluster into one strong peak near 2.5–3.2 kHz (Sundberg 1974). The ear is especially sensitive there, and the orchestra is relatively quiet. The green zone on the bar marks this region. Ring is perceived when higher-frequency resonances coordinate in this pocket.

    The RING meter compares the level near 3 kHz with what an ordinary speaking voice would produce on the same vowel (Sundberg 1995).

    0 dB
    Speech-like.
    +6 dB or more
    A singer's formant is present.

    The reading rises somewhat with loudness and is approximate on high notes. In Detail view the bar shows the spectrum from 2 to 4 kHz on its side. The filled shape is your voice; the dotted line is the speech-like reference.

    Vowel · F1 × F2

    The map is laid out like the IPA vowel chart: front vowels on the left, close vowels at the top. The circles are the seven Italian vowels for your voice range, or your own vowels after calibration.

    Resonance tuning

    Trained singers adjust their resonances as they go higher so that a harmonic stays on a resonance:

    • F1 rises to follow the note once the note reaches it.
    • High voices raise F2 to the second harmonic.
    • Low voices tune F1 to the second harmonic near the top of the range.

    The vowel circles move in the same way as you sing higher. Dotted lines (H1, H2, H3) mark the harmonics.

    Input

    Shows the microphone level in dB relative to digital full scale, not sound pressure. CLIP means the signal reached its maximum; the sound is distorted and the readings are paused. Lower the microphone's gain or step back.

    音高

    大字母是最接近的音名。颜色表示你的音准，依据约 ½ 秒内的平均音高判断，因此颤音不会使它闪烁。

    绿色
    与该音相差 10 音分以内：音准。
    橙色
    相差 30 音分以内：略高或略低。
    灰色
    介于两个音之间。

    1 音分为半音的 1/100。

    颤音

    速率
    每秒音高起伏的次数（Hz）。阴影带 5.5–6.7 Hz 是在十位著名歌剧演唱家身上测得的范围（Prame 1994）。6 Hz 处的刻度是听众认为最合适的速率（Anand 等 2012）。
    幅度
    音高向上、向下各偏离多少，以音分计。职业歌手约为 ±34 至 ±123 音分，平均 ±71（Prame 1997）。过宽的颤音被认为不太合适。

    颤音在音的结尾处会自然加快。很短的音不足以完成一个完整周期（6 Hz 时约 0.17 秒）。

    共振峰

    共振峰是声道（咽喉、口腔与双唇）的共振。歌剧演唱者可以调控声道的共振来改变自己的声音。

    F0
    不是共振峰，而是基频，即你唱的音高。
    F1
    下颌张开时升高：[i]、[u] 低，[a] 高。
    F2
    舌位前移时升高：[i] 高，[u] 低。
    F3
    双唇收圆时降低。

    只有当声音的某个谐波（F0 的整数倍）落在共振峰上时，这个共振峰才能被听到。

    F3 · 共鸣

    共鸣（ring），又称歌手共振峰或 squillo（意大利语，意为"嘹亮、号角般"），是训练有素的嗓音明亮、穿透力强、能越过乐队被听见的特质。它的成因是 F3、F4、F5 聚集成 2.5–3.2 kHz 附近的一个强峰（Sundberg 1974）。人耳在这一频段格外敏感，而乐队在这里相对较弱。竖条上的绿色区域标出这一频段。当较高频率的共振在这一频段协同聚集时，就能听到共鸣。

    共鸣表比较 3 kHz 附近的能量与普通说话声在同一元音上的能量（Sundberg 1995）。

    0 dB
    接近说话声。
    +6 dB 及以上
    具有歌手共振峰。

    声音越响，读数会略有升高；高音时读数只是近似值。在详细视图中，竖条横向显示 2–4 kHz 的频谱：填充形状是你的声音，虚线是说话声的参考。

    元音 · F1 × F2

    此图的布局与国际音标元音图相同：前元音在左，闭元音在上。圆圈是你所选音域的七个意大利语元音；校准后则显示你自己的元音。

    共振调谐

    训练有素的歌手在唱高时会调整共振，使某个谐波始终落在共振上：

    • 当音高达到 F1 时，F1 随音高一起上升。
    • 高音声部把 F2 提高到第二谐波。
    • 低音声部在音域顶端把 F1 调到第二谐波。

    随着你唱高，元音圆圈也会以同样方式移动。虚线（H1、H2、H3）标出各谐波。

    输入

    显示麦克风电平，单位是相对于数字满刻度的 dB，而不是声压级。CLIP 表示信号达到最大值：声音失真，读数暂停。请调低麦克风增益或离远一些。
