    How to read the screen

    Pitch

    The big letter is the nearest note. Green = within 10¢, orange = within 30¢, grey = between notes. The colour uses your pitch averaged over ~½ s, so vibrato doesn't flicker it; the trace shows the raw pitch so you can see vibrato width and evenness.

    F3 · ring

    The third formant. Lip rounding lowers it, so the pointer moves toward rounded. The labels show the unrounded/rounded pair for the vowel you're singing (e.g. ʌ / ɔ). The green zone around 3 kHz is the singer's formant region; RING compares energy at 2.5–3.5 kHz with 0.3–2 kHz.

    Vowel · F1 × F2

    Laid out like the IPA chart: front vowels left, close vowels top. Circles are the seven Italian vowels for your voice type, or your own after Calibrate. A formant is only audible where a harmonic (a multiple of F0) lands on it. Above ~300 Hz the analyzer fits the harmonic peaks directly; once F0 rises above a vowel's F1 (≈400 Hz for i and u) there is no harmonic below F1 to reveal it, so closed vowels read as more open — the same vowel blending listeners hear on high notes.

    Follow pitch

    Trained singers move their resonances as they sing higher so a harmonic stays on a resonance: F1 is raised to follow the note once the note passes it, high voices pull F2 up to the 2nd harmonic, and low voices tune F1 to the 2nd harmonic near the top of their range. With Follow pitch on, the circles move this way; faint ghosts mark their low-pitch positions and dotted lines (H1, H2, H3) mark the harmonics. Where vowels merge, every circle the dot is inside lights up — at that pitch the sound can't tell them apart.

    Synthesizer

    Plays a synthesized vowel with exactly the pitch (F0), formants (F1–F3) and volume you set, and runs it through the analyzer. Use it to hear what a target vowel sounds like, or to check the displays against known values. The vowel buttons load the current landmarks.

    Input

    Microphone level in dB relative to digital full scale (not SPL). Stay in the green; CLIP means the signal hit maximum and readings will be distorted.

    Sources
